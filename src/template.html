Showdown

An anime card duel built on Yu-Gi-Oh style rules: face-down monsters, Tribute Summons, traps and chains.

Your deck
How to play
  • Win by taking the opponent to 0 Life Points (you both start with 8000). Running out of cards to draw loses.
  • Each turn: draw, Main Phase 1, Battle Phase, Main Phase 2. Hand limit is 7 (discard down at end of turn). The first turn of the duel has no attack.
  • Monsters: Normal Summon or Set one per turn. Level 5-6 needs 1 Tribute, Level 7+ needs 2.
  • Set monsters sit face-down in Defense. Next turn you can Flip Summon them, or they flip when attacked. Flip effects trigger when they turn face-up.
  • Position: change a monster's position once per turn, but not the turn it was placed and not after it attacked.
  • Attacks: each monster attacks once per turn. Attack vs Attack: higher ATK wins and the difference is damage. Attack vs Defense: ATK vs DEF, no damage unless Pierce.
  • Direct attacks are only allowed when the opponent has no monster that can defend. Cannot Defend monsters do not count.
  • Chains: when a card or effect is activated, the other player may respond with a face-down Trap (Set last turn or earlier) or Quick-Play Spell. Links resolve last in, first out. Counter Traps can answer anything; normal Traps cannot answer a Counter Trap.
  • Golden rule: a card's text beats the base rules. Some cards attack more often, raise the hand limit, or allow extra Summons.
